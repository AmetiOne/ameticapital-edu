Calculations run in your browser. This release does not upload worksheet inputs or save values in browser storage. Reloading resets the examples. There is no account system or enquiry form on this site.

Cloudflare may deliver this site and may process IP addresses, browser information and access logs, and use security cookies to operate and protect its services. No advertising, analytics, external fonts or affiliate-tracking scripts are included in this release.

If you choose to email us at contact@ameticapital.com, your mail provider transmits the message and we receive the details you include. External websites have their own privacy practices. Links to sibling educational pages on ameti.capital are ordinary hyperlinks, not redirects from this domain.

Requests
