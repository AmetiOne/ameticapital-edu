Privacy notice

This notice explains what information is processed when you use ameticapital.com, operated by Ameti One LLC. Last updated November 9, 2026.

Calculator inputs

The calculators run entirely in your browser using JavaScript. The numbers you type are not sent to us or stored on a server. Reloading the page resets them. The "Download calculation" button creates a text file on your own device; we never receive it.

Accounts, advertising and analytics

The site has no sign-up, no login, no advertising and no affiliate links. Analytics. This site uses Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-5EM8PQN10G) to measure aggregate page views and site usage. Google Analytics sets first-party cookies to distinguish browsers and sends page-view metadata to Google. Calculator inputs are never sent to Google Analytics or any server — all calculations and downloaded records run entirely in your browser. We do not run advertising networks, remarketing pixels, or cross-site behavioral profiling.

Hosting and security processing

The site is served through Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes technical request data, such as IP address, browser user agent, requested URL and time, to deliver pages and protect against abuse, and it may run security or bot-detection checks. That processing is governed by Cloudflare's own privacy policy. We do not use this data to profile visitors.

Email

If you email [email protected], we receive your address and message and use them only to reply and to act on your request (for example, fixing an error). Messages are delivered through our email-forwarding and mailbox providers.

Links to other sites

Guides link to external sources such as OpenStax and the U.S. Small Business Administration, and to our sibling site ameti.capital. Those sites have their own privacy practices.

Children

The site is intended for a general adult audience learning business arithmetic and is not directed at children.

Changes and contact

If our practices change, for example if advertising were ever added or the analytics setup changed, this notice will be updated before the change takes effect. Questions: contact page.
