Privacy notice

This notice explains what information is processed when you use ameticapital.com, operated by Ameti One LLC. Last updated October 4, 2026.

Calculator inputs

The calculators run entirely in your browser using JavaScript. The numbers you type are not sent to us or stored on a server. Reloading the page resets them. The "Download calculation" button creates a text file on your own device; we never receive it.

No accounts, advertising or analytics scripts

The site has no sign-up, no login, no advertising, no affiliate links and no third-party analytics or tracking scripts, and the site's own code does not set cookies.

Hosting and security processing

The site is served through Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes technical request data, such as IP address, browser user agent, requested URL and time, to deliver pages and protect against abuse, and it may run security or bot-detection checks. That processing is governed by Cloudflare's own privacy policy. We do not use this data to profile visitors.

Email

If you email contact@ameticapital.com, we receive your address and message and use them only to reply and to act on your request (for example, fixing an error). Messages are delivered through our email-forwarding and mailbox providers.

Links to other sites

Guides link to external sources such as OpenStax and the U.S. Small Business Administration, and to our sibling site ameti.capital. Those sites have their own privacy practices.

Children

The site is intended for a general adult audience learning business arithmetic and is not directed at children.

Changes and contact

If our practices change, for example if analytics or advertising were ever added, this notice will be updated before the change takes effect. Questions: contact page.
