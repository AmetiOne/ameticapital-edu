Spelling and soft-collision notice

Our publisher name uses the spelling Ameti (with an e). This educational hub is distinct from unrelated advisory brands that use similar spellings such as Amati (with an a). We do not provide capital advisory, succession planning or M&A services, and we are not affiliated with those unrelated firms.

How the content is prepared

Content is prepared with AI assistance and checked for internal consistency, visible assumptions and functioning links. Calculators expose their formulas and are tested against worked examples. We do not claim professional credentials, independent product testing, customer outcomes or first-hand case studies that have not been documented.

Evidence and changes

Arithmetic examples are our own illustrations unless a primary reference is linked. A publication context records when a page was prepared; it is not a claim that every external source changed that day. Report a correction with the page URL and enough detail to reproduce the issue via contact.
