About ameticapital.com and our editorial method

ameticapital.com is a free educational site about break-even analysis, contribution margin and cost behavior. It is published by Ameti One LLC. It is educational only, not an investment, capital advisory, lending, succession-planning or M&A service.

Spelling and soft-collision notice

Our name uses the spelling Ameti (with an e). This site is not affiliated with unrelated firms that use similar names, including the Canadian advisory brand Amati Capital (with an a). We do not provide advisory, financing, succession or transaction services of any kind, and nothing here should be read as an offer of such services.

What we publish

  • Calculators for break-even and contribution margin that run entirely in your browser.
  • Guides that each explain one cost-volume-profit question, with a formula, a worked numeric example and links to the sources for the definitions.

Margin, markup and pricing-from-margin worksheets are published on a separate sibling site, ameti.capital. The two sites are connected by ordinary text links; neither redirects to the other.

How content is prepared and checked

  1. Definitions from standard sources. Formulas follow standard managerial-accounting usage, and each guide links its references, mainly OpenStax Principles of Accounting, Volume 2: Managerial Accounting and U.S. Small Business Administration guidance.
  2. Illustrative numbers only. Worked examples use round, invented figures (for example a $40 product with $25 of variable cost). They are labeled as illustrative and are not data about any real business, industry average or customer.
  3. Arithmetic checked twice. Each worked example is recomputed independently, and the calculators are tested against the same examples so the page and the tool agree.
  4. AI assistance disclosed. Drafting is done with AI assistance; a review step checks formulas, links and claims before publication. We do not claim professional credentials, case studies, testimonials or client results.

Corrections and updates

If you find an error, such as a wrong figure, a broken link or an unclear definition, email us via the contact page with the page URL and what you expected. Confirmed errors are fixed and the page's "last reviewed" date is updated. We do not change worked examples without updating that date.

Funding and advertising

There are no advertisements, affiliate links or tracking scripts on this site. If that ever changes, this page and the privacy notice will be updated first.
